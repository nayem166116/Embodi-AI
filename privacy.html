Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Embodi AI, Inc. ("Embodi", "we", "us") provides embodied AI software for industrial automation. This policy explains what we collect on this website and through deployed products, and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

Website usage: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected via analytics cookies described in our Cookie Policy.

Account and trial information: name, work email, company, and any details submitted through the free trial or demo request forms.

Deployment data: with a signed customer agreement, camera imagery and process telemetry captured on your production line, used strictly to configure and support your own deployment.

2. How we use information

  • To operate and improve this website and the Automation Feasibility Estimator tool.
  • To respond to trial, demo, and support requests.
  • To configure, monitor, and support a customer's specific deployment.
  • To meet legal, security, and contractual obligations.

3. Deployment data is never shared across customers

Camera and process data captured on a customer's line is scoped to that customer's deployment. It is not pooled with other customers' data and is not used to train models shared outside that deployment without separate written agreement.

4. Data retention

Website analytics data is retained for up to 24 months. Deployment data retention is set out in each customer's service agreement and can be reduced or deleted on request, subject to contractual and legal requirements.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact privacy@embodi.ai to exercise these rights.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@embodi.ai.
