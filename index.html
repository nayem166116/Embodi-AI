// 04 — how it works

From pilot cell to full line in four steps

STEP / 01

Capture & calibrate

We mount cameras on your existing cell and capture a short data pass of real parts and motions.

STEP / 02

Train perception

Embodi Vision and Skill Learning tune to your SKUs, tolerances, and lighting — typically in under two weeks.

STEP / 03

Shadow run

Task Agents run in shadow mode alongside your current process, scored against ground truth before go-live.

STEP / 04

Go live & scale

Fleet Orchestrator rolls the trained skill out to additional cells and lines with one control plane.

// 08 — faq

Questions plant teams actually ask

Does Embodi AI replace our existing robot controllers?

+

No. Embodi AI runs alongside your existing PLCs and robot controllers as a perception and decision layer, sending standard motion commands your hardware already understands.

What robot brands and cameras are supported?

+

Embodi Vision integrates with the major industrial arms and AMR platforms plus standard GigE and RGB-D camera lines. Our deployment team confirms compatibility during the design review.

How long does a pilot cell take to go live?

+

Most pilot cells complete capture, training and shadow-run validation in five to eight weeks, depending on part variability and camera access on your line.

Is the Feasibility Estimator's output a guarantee?

+

No. It's a directional, public preview tool modeled on patterns from past deployments. A binding assessment comes from a design review with our deployment engineers.

How is our production data handled?

+

Camera and process data stay scoped to your deployment and are never used to train models shared across customers. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.
