This Cookie Policy explains how Embodi AI, Inc. uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. What cookies we use

Essential cookies keep the site functioning, including remembering your cookie consent choice via local storage.

Analytics cookies help us understand which pages and products visitors find useful, so we can prioritize what to build next. These are only set if you accept them in the cookie banner.

2. Managing your preference

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies from the banner shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site and reloading the page.

We do not sell data collected through cookies. Analytics providers we use are bound by data processing agreements limiting their use of the data to providing the analytics service to us.
